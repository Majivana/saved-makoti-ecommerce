Where quality meets class

Designed to preserve heritage, and invoke confidence.

Saved Makoti Clothing Line began with a simple belief: that traditional Xhosa bridal wear deserves the same craft and consideration as any global fashion house. Every dress that leaves our studio at 49 Leeds Rd is cut, beaded and finished by hand — built for the umgcagco, and for the woman who wears it long after the ceremony ends.
