Founded on heritage

Designed to preserve heritage, and invoke confidence.

Saved Makoti Clothing Line was founded by Achuma Mandungwana in Mthatha, in the heart of the Eastern Cape — home to the Xhosa traditions the brand draws from. What began as a small collection of made-to-order umbhaco pieces for family and friends has grown into a studio trusted by brides across South Africa for their umgcagco and umembeso ceremonies.

Every Saved Makoti piece is still cut, beaded and finished by hand in our Leeds Road studio. We believe a Makoti outfit should do two things at once: honour the generations of women who wore these patterns before, and make the woman wearing it today feel unmistakably herself.

Our Mission & Values

Three principles guide every dress that leaves the studio.

Authenticity

Rooted in real Xhosa Makoti tradition — the colours, cuts and beadwork worn for generations, never a costume interpretation.

Quality

Every bead is placed by hand. Every seam is finished to be worn hard on the happiest day of your life, and for years after.

Empowerment

A Saved Makoti bride should feel grounded and confident — dressed in her heritage, not costumed by it.
